# 🐽 Oink and Fly

**For solo travellers who want to know: *where can I actually afford to go?***

Drop in your savings, your monthly situation, and how many days you want to disappear for — and Oink and Fly shows you exactly which destinations fit your budget, with a full cost breakdown so you can see the math.

No sign-up. No tracking. Everything runs in your browser.

🌍 **[Try it → rmitra97.github.io/oinkandfly](https://rmitra97.github.io/oinkandfly/)**

---

## What it does right now

- Calculates your real travel budget from savings + income (with a 3-month safety net built in)
- Matches you to affordable destinations across 40+ cities worldwide
- Shows flight estimates, daily costs, and total trip cost as a range — never fake-precise numbers
- Filters by trip length, travel style (Shoestring → Treat Yourself), and how far you want to roam
- Clickable city cards with seasonal tips, local transport advice, and booking links

## Work in progress 🚧

More features, more cities, and more ways to explore on the way. This is an early version — lots more to come.
